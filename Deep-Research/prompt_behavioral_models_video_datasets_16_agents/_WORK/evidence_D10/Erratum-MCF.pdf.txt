Prepared by: Miao Feng, Jean Meunier 
Date: Nov. 28, 2025 
Introduction 
The Multiple-Camera Fall (MCF) dataset and its accompanying technical report have been 
valuable resources for research within our laboratory. During continued use and validation, we 
identified several documentation inconsistencies, most notably regarding frame-delay values, 
reference cameras, and temporal-segmentation frame ranges. To ensure accurate interpretation 
and support future research using this dataset, the following erratum provides corrected values 
for clarity and consistency. 
1. Overview of Issues 
● Frame delay values of camera 5 for scenario 5. 
● Camera reference 10 and 11 inside tables at pages 17-22. 
● Period segmentation boundaries inside tables at pages 17-22.

3. Notes for Dataset Users 
● These corrections apply to technical report only, raw video data remains unchanged. 
● Researchers performing scenario segmentation or synchronization should use this erratum 
to avoid misalignment. 
● This clarification is recommended for all future work based on the MCF dataset.
