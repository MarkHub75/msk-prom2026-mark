# Rapid detector adaptation for industrial CCTV: conditional decisions and usable artifacts

The useful outcome is a selected, reproducible student bundle whose critical quality and actual LinQ execution are demonstrated. Start with zero-update reference inference and the earliest unresolved task/data/observation gate, then choose one intervention that distinguishes plausible causes of error. A working bundle should enter integration while separate candidate revisions are explored. The sequence is **proposed**, not a completed training result. [inference/proposed: F007](RAW_FACTS.md#f007), [F282–F284](RAW_FACTS.md#f282), [E051](RAW_FACTS.md#e051).

## D01 Ready weights, baseline and object-versus-event error attribution

Ready weights suffice only when outputs express the required task, independent target material supports agreed critical constraints and the final artifact executes through the real LinQ path. Without target GT, establish feasibility and visible errors; sufficient quality remains **unknown**. Obtain permitted complete diagnostic labels before interpreting open-data fine-tuning as the best next step. [inference/proposed: F007](RAW_FACTS.md#f007), [E001](RAW_FACTS.md#e001); [unknown: Q001](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q001), [Q004](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q004), [Q010](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q010).

Detection supplies objects/boxes. PPE wearing requires state and association; zone entry needs geometry/person reference point; smoking may require hand/object/temporal context. Keep object TP/FP/FN separate from person/track, relations, event intervals and alerts. On identical development clips compare A=ordinary pipeline; B=oracle GT objects with unchanged association/rules; optionally C=oracle relations too. A→B diagnoses detector contribution, B→C association, residual C temporal rules/observability/task. These substitutions remove upstream errors deliberately; they are diagnostic, not deployable improvements or an estimate of achievable training gain. Save all substituted stage outputs. [inference/proposed: F002](RAW_FACTS.md#f002), [F082](RAW_FACTS.md#f082), [E002](RAW_FACTS.md#e002); [unknown: Q029](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q029).

TIDE categories are classification, localisation, both, duplicates, background and missed GT; paper foreground/background IoU thresholds are `0.5/0.1`. Each oracle ΔAP starts from the original result; sequential fixes change apparent contributions. Missed GT does not identify whether pixels, labels, resize, class coverage or adaptation caused the miss. These are diagnostic settings, not alert thresholds. [docs/code: F001–F003](RAW_FACTS.md#f001); [primary TIDE paper §§2.1–2.3](https://dbolya.github.io/tide/paper.pdf).

For E001 preserve checkpoint/config hashes; `class_id→name→definition`; eval mode; frame decode/color/normalisation; resize/letterbox/padding and inverse coordinates; actual tensor dtype/layout/shape/hash; raw output schema/decoder; confidence/NMS/top-k/`max_det`; event rules. Specify data/split explicitly: Ultralytics validation can fall back to `coco8.yaml`; rectangular validation and fixed export may use different geometry. Inspect original, post-transform image and correctly aligned GT/prediction overlays. Wrong classes/channels/coordinates stop quality interpretation. If evidence disappears only after preprocessing, investigate input geometry. If humans cannot identify the state in the original, investigate observation/unknown/temporal requirements; more epochs have no established basis. This is not proof that every model must fail. [docs/inference/proposed: F006–F007](RAW_FACTS.md#f006), [F080](RAW_FACTS.md#f080), [E003](RAW_FACTS.md#e003).

## D02 Pretraining selection: representation, detector tensors and source-stage confounds

Choose initialization by actual transferable tensors, semantic coverage, provenance, compatible topology and cost to obtain a trainable artifact. Representation pretraining, localisation pretext training and supervised source detector training are distinct. DetCo's converter provides representation, not a trained predictor. DETReg `args.pretrain` removes `class_embed` while potentially retaining bbox/transformer/query/input-projection components beyond its SwAV backbone; FrozenBN ancestry matters. A new classifier does not erase transferred localisation; matching class-row shape or text does not establish PPE wearing semantics. [code/docs/inference: F234–F236](RAW_FACTS.md#f234), [F240](RAW_FACTS.md#f240); [DetCo v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.04803v2), [DETReg v5 §§4.2–4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2106.04550v5).

DETReg reports `13.7/22.6 nAP` at `10/30-shot` with supervised base stage versus `9.1/16.5` without it. This is not an isolated source-stage effect: schedules differ (`150 epochs/drop140` versus `1000/drop990`), with different exposure/supervision packages. Asset filename `scratch` means no supervised base-class stage, not no pretraining. Few-shot results/assets were updated in July 2023 under a 2021 release label; tag URL is not tensor hash. In-domain pretraining saw all COCO train2017 unlabelled, beyond the `1%` labelled subset. [publishedmeasurement/code: F234](RAW_FACTS.md#f234), [F237–F240](RAW_FACTS.md#f237).

Historical Mask R-CNN R50-FPN+GroupNorm evidence: on `10k COCO images`, pretrained `26.0 bbox AP/60k iterations`, scratch `25.9/220k`; on `1k`, pretrained `9.9`, scratch `3.5` in text/`3.4` in caption, `5.4` after scratch-specific hyperparameter search. Preserve the internal 3.4/3.5 discrepancy. Convergence cost and data regime matter; these measurements neither prohibit scratch universally nor define target gains. [publishedmeasurement: F011](RAW_FACTS.md#f011); [primary paper §5.2, Figs.7–8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.08883).

When detector-specific initialization is the main uncertainty and artifacts are ready, use **E048 instead of the limited/full pair**: identical backbone, classifier initialization, data and downstream recipe; change only selected compatible extra detector tensors. Record key/hash/load/skip reports and old-domain retention. Different author schedules/ancestry cannot form a clean weight-only ablation. Ready assets still need rights, retrieval/conversion and locking; pretraining from scratch adds unlabelled data/proposals/cache/teacher/dependencies/compute. Unlabelled video alone does not justify it. Missing exact assets or clean comparison → retain a suitable pretrained reference and close [Q005](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q005)/[Q015](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q015)/[Q030](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q030), rather than invent a ranking. [proposed/inference: F239](RAW_FACTS.md#f239), [E048](RAW_FACTS.md#e048).

## D03 Small labelled sets, few-shot units, limited/full fine-tuning and BatchNorm

Use a small **fully checked** target set to test one limited-versus-full adaptation pair from the same target-initialized state; its size alone does not choose freeze depth. Count images, instances/class, independent positive episodes/tracks, cameras/sessions and ordinary video hours separately. Adjacent frames increase exposure, not independent diversity. Construct groups before selection/crops/pseudo/synthetic generation; derivatives inherit source roles. [inference/proposed: F032](RAW_FACTS.md#f032), [F100](RAW_FACTS.md#f100), [E004–E006](RAW_FACTS.md#e004).

TFA shot is **object instance/class**, not frame or independent scene. Base detector training precedes balanced base+novel predictor adaptation with fixed feature components. Faster R-CNN R101-FPN on COCO novel `10/30 shots`: TFA cosine `10.0/13.7 nAP`, its full fine-tuning baseline `9.2/12.5`; AP points, not AP50 or event recall. Reported recipe: SGD batch16, momentum0.9, weight decay0.0001, base LR0.02, few-shot LR0.001. Revised protocol uses `10` COCO and `30` VOC training-shot groups; Fig.3 separately shows variability up to `40 runs`. Shot-set variance differs from optimizer-seed variance. DeFRCN changes gradients/architecture and updates more components; inference PCB adds another intervention. TFA freezes backbone/RPN/RoI features; arbitrary `freeze=10` in another detector is another intervention. None is a generic YOLO freeze recipe. [publishedmeasurement/docs: F008–F012](RAW_FACTS.md#f008); [TFA §3/Table3](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/wang20j/wang20j.pdf).

In Ultralytics U/8.4.184, `BaseModel.load` compatible tensor intersection and normalized-name remapping can transfer class rows after strip/lower while excluding placeholders; changing `nc` does not reset the entire head automatically. `DetectionTrainer.get_model` builds with dataset classes/channels, sets names then loads; inspect **after trainer setup**, including `head.nc`, not just the pre-`train()` object. Fused inference weights are not equivalent to an unfused training checkpoint. [code: F016–F017](RAW_FACTS.md#f016); [pinned tasks.py](https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics/blob/e6d5fc5918f57d4d39fadb43fc5b2a1aea132ac8/ultralytics/nn/tasks.py).

In U, `freeze=N` expands to `range(N)`, `.dfl` stays frozen, and trainer may re-enable floating parameters outside the mask. `_model_train` sets train mode, then matching frozen `BatchNorm2d` to eval each epoch, freezing those running statistics. Frozen parameters and frozen BN are different contracts; this trace does not establish SyncBN/custom-normalization behaviour. E005 separates BN-statistic changes from feature-weight updates **only when drift/train-eval evidence motivates that arm**. [code: F013–F018](RAW_FACTS.md#f013), [F276](RAW_FACTS.md#f276).

E004 controls initialization/class rows, manifests, actual optimizer, transforms, successful updates/exposure and selection rule; create a new class head identically before branching. Verify mask/BN/update/save/reload first. Compare critical/old-domain metrics on independent grouped dev data. Critical regression → rollback; small/sign-changing effect → one bounded paired repeat or new independent labels according to the uncertainty source. Keep `training_sample_seed`, `optimization_seed`, split hash and resampling seed separate. Frozen features can fail under domain shift; full adaptation can overfit. Neither one successful seed nor more neighboring frames resolves absent support. [proposed: E004](RAW_FACTS.md#e004), [E005](RAW_FACTS.md#e005), [E006](RAW_FACTS.md#e006), [E047](RAW_FACTS.md#e047); [unknown: Q014](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q014), [Q026](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q026).

## D04 Annotation selection and active learning: scalar Results are not entropy

Choose the next independent examples under a measured full human-time budget. Test one available selector against stratified random or informed manual selection with the same annotation-completeness rule and total minutes: selection, drawing/correction, miss search, QA/adjudication, import/export. Counts of images, boxes and minutes are different budgets. In a Faster R-CNN/BDD100K practical study, curves were close and objectness selection's small gain involved approximately `+40%` annotated objects; human-time savings were not measured. Another video-AL study had random ahead after the first BDD100K cycle. [publishedmeasurement/inference: F095–F097](RAW_FACTS.md#f095); [primary practical evaluation §§5–7](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-3259/ialatecml_paper5.pdf).

**Interface constraint, Ultralytics U/8.4.184:** `DetectionPredictor` detect `Results` has original-image `xyxy`, scalar `conf/cls`; `probs=None`; `boxes.data` is already postprocessing output. No raw class-logit vector, background probabilities or full class distribution is exposed there. `1-conf` is a retained-low-confidence heuristic, not entropy or calibrated uncertainty. Lowering prediction conf restores neither discarded class vectors nor NMS/cap losses. `model.embed` returns image vectors through a path skipping ordinary detection postprocess; simultaneous Results+embedding needs another forward or custom extraction. [code: F220–F225](RAW_FACTS.md#f220); [Results/Boxes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ultralytics/ultralytics/e6d5fc5918f57d4d39fadb43fc5b2a1aea132ac8/ultralytics/engine/results.py).

Minimal **proposed** selector F226, when the pool has **already been inferred**: nonempty frame `u=1-min_b(conf_b)`; no boxes → `null`, not zero uncertainty. Mix ranking with seeded metadata exploration covering high-confidence and no-box frames; temporal/group exclusion spans all acquisition rounds. Dense images have more opportunities for a low score and may cost more to annotate. Log every frame, including empty/error/skipped; group/time/hash; retained boxes; possible truncation at `count==max_det`; inference/selector arguments; selection reason. Zero extra forwards applies only to scoring these existing outputs; pool inference/NMS/serialization and human QA still cost time. Unknown/out-of-taxonomy objects and confident systematic errors require exploration. [proposed/code: F226](RAW_FACTS.md#f226), [E022](RAW_FACTS.md#e022).

Active Teacher uses Faster R-CNN ROI logits; its diversity is the number of distinct predicted classes **inside an image**, not interframe distance or temporal deduplication. Embeddings, ensembles and MC-dropout need separate interface/cost justification. Keep acquisition/mining pool out of independent evaluation. If the selected arm costs more without independent useful gain, keep simple selection and the reviewed labels. E043 compares independent real-label addition with retaining reference or another compute intervention under the actually available budget. A first annotation benefit may be making critical evaluation possible at all. [docs/inference/proposed: F093–F094](RAW_FACTS.md#f093), [F100–F101](RAW_FACTS.md#f100), [E043](RAW_FACTS.md#e043); [unknown: Q012](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q012), [Q016](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q016).

## D05 Pseudo-label admission: blind complete GT, teacher misses, sampling and support N/A

Start with teacher-assisted annotation versus manual work, then fully corrected labels for supervised adaptation. Unchecked pseudo-labels require a separate competence/admission decision; no target teacher stratum has passed it here. A high score is not GT or proof of wearing/event semantics. Confidence calibration estimates prediction correctness under a specified class+IoU match; a missed GT has no score to calibrate. `netcal` requires caller-supplied binary `matched` per prediction. Calibration cannot recover missed-positive recall. [docs/code/inference: F227–F233](RAW_FACTS.md#f227); [unknown: Q011](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q011).

**E024 audit design, proposed:**

1. Define permitted train-only pool, group/frame sampling units, match/duplicate/IoU rules, class×size×camera strata, acceptance constraints and observation exposure. P is a seeded probability sample with known inclusion probabilities; D targets rare predictions, tiny/occluded objects, disagreement, high/low scores, empty outputs and human-found misses. A predicted rare class is not a true rare class. D discovers failures but cannot be pooled into population rates when its inclusion probabilities are unknown.
2. On the blind complete-GT portion, reviewers label **all** objects within task scope before seeing teacher overlays, including no-detection frames. Keep unknown/ignore states. Match one-to-one using the fixed rule; distinguish GT removed by cutoff from GT lacking a usable original detection.
3. With known design, `w_i=1/pi_i`; proposed ratio estimators are `precision_hat=sum(w_i TP_i)/sum(w_i(TP_i+FP_i))` and `retained_recall_hat=sum(w_i TP_i)/sum(w_i GT_i)`. Finite-sample ratios are not guaranteed unbiased. Variance must respect clustering/stratification. D without pi supports diagnostic counts, not whole-pool rates. `accepted/candidates` is not recall.
4. Select cutoff/prompt/teacher on calibration groups, freeze policy, then use separate complete-GT confirmation groups. Changing policy after confirmation makes those groups development data. Report accepted/TP/FP/FN/GT, localization, independent positive groups and exposure by stratum.
5. `GT_c=0` → recall N/A. `accepted=0, GT>0` → precision N/A, recall 0. Degenerate bootstrap/NaN → interval unavailable; observing all successes does not justify a zero-width evidential interval. No universal minimum frames/groups, quality floor or tolerated loss is established; [Q010](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q010)/[Q011](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q011) require case-specific decisions. Unsupported critical strata cannot enter unchecked supervision.

[proposed with docs/code basis: E024](RAW_FACTS.md#e024), [F227–F233](RAW_FACTS.md#f227); [primary calibration §3.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.13546v1), [sampling-design methodological source](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/tutorials/sampledesign.aspx).

**Assistance cost E023:** assign comparable train-only groups to manual/teacher-assisted work under equal finished-label quality. The same person annotating the same image twice creates memory bias. Count searching misses, correction, adjudication and export QA, not accepted-click count. CVAT pinned driver default `clear_existing=False` adds annotations; `allow_unmatched_labels=False` requires mapping. Successful import does not inspect image truth; documentation `5–15%` review is not a statistical guarantee for rare classes. Analytics/Automated QA availability depends on deployment/Online/Enterprise and is unknown for this team. Savings with preserved quality → corrected labels/E025; no savings → manual or teacher only for hypotheses. [code/docs/proposed: F098–F099](RAW_FACTS.md#f098), [E023](RAW_FACTS.md#e023), [E025](RAW_FACTS.md#e025).

Even a competent teacher must improve the **student** under controlled comparison. Ordinary supervised loss can turn filtered-out positives into background; use full correction or a verified ignore/SSOD contract. A high cutoff can trade precision for retained recall, not guarantee benefit. Soft Teacher is not just filtered TXT: it uses distinct classification/background weights and regression uncertainty. Paper versus pinned code differ: uniform `±6%` jitter versus `torch.randn×box_scale×frac`; LR drops `110k/160k` versus `120k/160k`. README `thr=5e-2/1e-3` is test score threshold, not teacher cutoff. Do not merge these into one reproduced recipe. [docs/code: F102–F107](RAW_FACTS.md#f102), [F230](RAW_FACTS.md#f230).

## D06 Teacher roles, native YOLO11 feature KD and one-stage online SSOD extraction

Use a teacher for one explicit role: corrected preannotation, acquisition, offline noisy labels, online EMA supervision or feature/logit/box distillation. Roles differ in supervision, state, cost and final graph. Teacher AP is not student AP; a VLM phrase score does not establish a canonical class/box/relationship. GroundingDINO Swin-T OGC outputs normalized `cxcywh`, max token sigmoid and phrases; `phrases2classes` chooses the first class string appearing as substring. Require explicit phrase→canonical mapping, unknown/review and full target-instance checking. Its reported T/Swin-T COCO zero-shot `48.4`/fine-tune `57.2 AP` is not PPE competence or an L-model result. [docs/code/publishedmeasurement: F127–F129](RAW_FACTS.md#f127).

Preserve training lineage: selected serialized student/EMA bytes+hash and parent rollback; architecture/head/YAML/custom modules; package/dependency/OS/device/driver records; source/data/class/coverage manifests and group roles; actual recipe/mask/BN policy; separate seeds; successful update/exposure/retry ledger; evaluator/constraints/tie/stop and winner epoch. Keep teacher/projector training dependencies distinct from final inference dependencies. [inference/proposed: F193](RAW_FACTS.md#f193), [E037](RAW_FACTS.md#e037).

Stop affected branch for leakage, invalid supervision/class map, nonfinite/missing update, reload/extraction failure, critical regression, unsupported target contract, agreed cost cap or no timely useful-artifact path. Missing rare GT → unsupported, not verified by overall AP. Small/sign-changing effects → provisional/inconclusive until a justified repeat/new groups, not promotion by best seed. Continue only when the next work distinguishes plausible causes and fits full critical path. Hand over a working bundle immediately with evidence/status; new ideas use separate revisions. Rollback restores **weights+preprocessing+class map+postprocessing+rules**, not only `.pt`. [inference/proposed: F184](RAW_FACTS.md#f184), [F280](RAW_FACTS.md#f280), [F284](RAW_FACTS.md#f284), [E051](RAW_FACTS.md#e051).

Final E037 after bundle freeze uses an agreed independent lockbox against frozen baseline; then actual target model-only latency/memory/placement and end-to-end decode/preprocess/queue/network/all stages/transfer/merge/events. Record fixed input/batch/concurrency/warmup/timing boundaries; p50/p95`ms/frame`, throughput, dropped frames, calls/frame and bytes/frame. Sum of mean stages is not asynchronous p95. Failure → full rollback; missing target execution → `target-unverified`. Success does not establish absent rare states, unseen-camera SLA or industrial safety.
