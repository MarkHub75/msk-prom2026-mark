# Choose the test from the claim, not from a metric name

Use four separate evidence layers: visible object → state of the correct person → independently defined violation episode → useful delivered alert. Actual execution on LinQ is an additional claim. Good object AP cannot establish correct ownership; segment overlap cannot establish timely delivery; fast graph execution cannot establish fresh live output. No supported universal safety score combines these layers. **[INFER]** [F019](RAW_FACTS.md#f019), [F044](RAW_FACTS.md#f044), [F061](RAW_FACTS.md#f061), [F146](RAW_FACTS.md#f146).

Evidence labels used here: **[DOC]** inspected primary documentation; **[CODE]** inspected source, not necessarily executed; **[PUB]** authors' published method/result, conditional on their setting; **[LOCAL]** actually executed small source-level/synthetic calculation; **[MATH]** constructed analytical example, not a system measurement; **[DERIVATION]** our mathematical derivation; **[INFER]** our supported inference; **[PROPOSED]** our future protocol, not an official rule; **[UNKNOWN]** unavailable input. A paragraph may combine labels; its F record preserves the individual bases. Current research did not measure a model's AP/recall/FPS, production video, LinQ execution or safety effectiveness. The named fixtures are evidence about their calculations only. **[LOCAL/UNKNOWN]** [F001](RAW_FACTS.md#f001).

The official brief requires LinQ inference and mentions helmet absence, removed respirator, dangerous-zone entry and smoking near combustible material as examples. Full mandatory taxonomy, evaluator, allowable latency, utility costs and numerical acceptance thresholds remain unavailable: [Q001](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q001). Official publication-to-upload window is 23 October 13:00 → 25 October 10:00, 45 hours; defense provides 2 minutes preparation + 5 minutes presentation + 3 minutes questions. Jury criteria do not imply an AP/FPS formula. **[DOC/UNKNOWN]** [F207](RAW_FACTS.md#f207), [F208](RAW_FACTS.md#f208), [S119](SOURCES.md#s119), [S120](SOURCES.md#s120).

## Ground truth: hidden PPE, missing labels and abstention are different

A missing helmet detection is not helmet absence. An absence label requires sufficient visible evidence of the relevant head region under a frozen manual. A hidden head is GT unknown; a model abstaining on a clearly visible, known violating head still misses a known violation. Technical unavailability, workflow-missing annotation and out-of-scope data need separate states. Pixels alone may not establish certification, respirator seal, hidden fastening, rule applicability or material combustibility. **[DOC/INFER/PROPOSED]** [F008](RAW_FACTS.md#f008)–[F018](RAW_FACTS.md#f018), [F046](RAW_FACTS.md#f046); unresolved criteria: [Q002](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q002), [Q003](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q003).

Keep two annotation axes: review scope `reviewed / not_reviewed / out_of_scope`; reviewed evidence `positive / negative / unknown`, separately per attribute. A known person can have unknown helmet state and known negative zone entry. `ignore` is a specific evaluator instruction with region/time and reason, not a universal encoding of unknown. Open Images box completeness is conditional on verified image labels; VOC, CrowdHuman and AVA have different visibility, omission and exclusion policies. Equal class names do not prove equal truth. AVA v2.2 distinguishes included empty timestamps from excluded timestamps; its revision changed labels, not just filenames. **[DOC/PROPOSED]** [F008](RAW_FACTS.md#f008)–[F015](RAW_FACTS.md#f015).

Store source hashes/lineage, decoded dimensions and timebase, review completeness, person/object IDs, visible versus optional amodal boxes, body-part visibility, physical/reflection/depiction status, PPE relations (`on_head/on_face`, held, hanging, nearby, owner_unknown), requirement version/context, episode evidence and onset/end bounds, gaps/censoring, identity certainty and raw/adjudicated annotation provenance. This is a proposed schema, not the supplied case format. **[PROPOSED]** [F014](RAW_FACTS.md#f014).

For state evaluation, retain all eligible GT person-time units `N_scope`, determinate positive/negative units `N_determinate`, unknown/not-reviewed/ignored counts and reason distributions. Observability coverage=`N_determinate/N_scope`. State whether units are person-frames or duration-weighted person-time; irregular frame counts are not duration. Empty denominator→NA with counts; no positives→recall NA. A reviewed empty scene contributes camera exposure but no person-state units. Conditional quality on visible/matched people must retain its restricted label. **[PROPOSED]** [F015](RAW_FACTS.md#f015), [F046](RAW_FACTS.md#f046).

Use a discussed pilot to refine the manual, then a fresh frozen-manual, prediction-blind independent pass by at least two annotators; save original responses before adjudication. Common-roster classification agreement and independent person finding/localization are different tasks. Publish the full contingency table, rare-state/unknown/workflow-missing counts and unmatched persons, not only kappa. For temporal agreement report jointly observed duration, episodes, boundary differences only where observable, and censoring. Mathematical example: 98 negative/negative agreements plus one positive/negative and one negative/positive among 100 units gives 98% raw agreement but no positive agreement and kappa≈−.0101. There is no universal project kappa/sample-size threshold. **[PUB/MATH/PROPOSED]** [F013](RAW_FACTS.md#f013), [F017](RAW_FACTS.md#f017); [E009](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#e009), [Q004](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q004).

## COCO bbox AP: exact score order, ignore, empty classes and caps

Pin the evaluator before comparing AP. The inspected reference is `cocodataset/cocoapi` commit `6c3b394c07aed33fd83784a8bf8798059a1e9ae4`; the studied `cocoeval.py` path revision is dated 2019-12-25. It is neither an installed organizer evaluator nor a full evaluator run. Use explicit `iouType='bbox'` (constructor default is `segm`). **[CODE]** [F020](RAW_FACTS.md#f020), [S008](SOURCES.md#s008).

- Input detections: `image_id`, `category_id`, `[x,y,width,height]`, `score`; continuous extents, no pixel-inclusive `+1`. Restore original-image coordinates after resize/letterbox. GT `area` is annotation-supplied and can be segmentation area even for bbox AP; detection area is width×height. Invalid dimensions, class mapping or normalized/xyxy inputs require wrapper validation. **[CODE]** [F021](RAW_FACTS.md#f021).
- Defaults: IoU `.50,.55,…,.95` (10); recall `0,.01,…,1` (101); `useCats=1`; `maxDets=[1,10,100]`. Squared-image-pixel ranges: all `[0,10^10]`, small `[0,1024]`, medium `[1024,9216]`, large `[9216,10^10]`. Tests exclude `<lower` or `>upper`, so 1024 and 9216 belong to both neighboring ranges. **[CODE]** [F022](RAW_FACTS.md#f022).
- Per image/category/IoU/area, stable descending-score sort and cap precede greedy highest qualifying IoU matching, favoring nonignored GT. Ordinary GT is consumed once; crowd can absorb several detections. Equality at threshold passes (`min(t,1−1e−10)` initialization); equal overlaps can replace an earlier candidate; score ties inherit input/concatenation order. This is not global Hungarian matching. **[CODE]** [F023](RAW_FACTS.md#f023).
- `_prepare` overwrites GT `ignore` with `iscrowd`; `ignore=1,iscrowd=0` does not create arbitrary ignore. Supply `iscrowd`. Area exclusion adds ignore separately. A detection matched to ignored GT, or unmatched outside the current area bin, contributes neither TP nor FP. Crowd overlap uses intersection/detection area, not ordinary IoU. Encoding all ambiguous PPE as crowd changes semantics. **[CODE]** [F024](RAW_FACTS.md#f024).
- Accumulation pools retained detections per class/area/cap across images and stable-sorts globally. Cumulative recall=`TP/nonignored_GT`; precision=`TP/(TP+FP+machine_spacing)`. Right-to-left precision envelope is sampled at 101 recall points, with unattained points zero; average over selected IoUs and valid classes. There is no TN denominator and this is not average frame precision or precision at threshold .5. **[CODE]** [F025](RAW_FACTS.md#f025).
- Zero nonignored GT for an entire class/area leaves sentinel −1, excluded from AP averaging even with false predictions. Report their counts/exposure separately. GT present with no predictions→AP/recall 0; both empty image/category→no TN credit; all-crowd→no positive denominator. An entirely empty result list breaks inspected `loadRes` at `anns[0]`; explicitly test an empty-result wrapper. **[CODE]** [F026](RAW_FACTS.md#f026).
- Standard 12 outputs: AP@[.50:.95], AP50, AP75, AP small/medium/large; AR@1/@10/@100 and AR small/medium/large@100. Raw fractions versus display percent must be labeled. `summarize` assumes defaults, partly hard-codes cap100 and partly reads `maxDets[2]`; custom caps/thresholds need explicitly selected/labeled tensor entries. With 101 same-class GT objects/image, cap100 limits recall to100/101. **[CODE/INFER]** [F027](RAW_FACTS.md#f027), [F031](RAW_FACTS.md#f031).

One GT with TP score .9 then duplicate .8 gives AP50≈1 but final thresholded precision1/2; FP .9 then TP .8 gives AP50=.5 with identical TP/FP totals. **[LOCAL]** These executed extracted `evaluateImg/accumulate` with supplied IoU matrices and a NumPy compatibility edit; loader, compiled IoU, raw-coordinate geometry and full summary were not executed. [F028](RAW_FACTS.md#f028). VOC can differ in interpolation, `+1`, strict overlap comparison and difficult handling; AP50 alone does not equalize protocols. **[CODE]** [F032](RAW_FACTS.md#f032). Keep AP diagnostics plus working-threshold TP/FP/FN and independent person/event outcomes; reproduce official scoring separately when supplied. **[PROPOSED]** [F033](RAW_FACTS.md#f033), [Q001](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q001).

## Person–PPE ownership: all known violating people remain in recall

Evaluate both GT-person-input state recognition and the complete predicted-person chain. Match people by geometry independently of predicted PPE state, then evaluate ownership/state; otherwise matching can conceal owner swaps. Match both endpoints before relation scoring, and distinguish worn-by from held-by. **[PROPOSED]** [F043](RAW_FACTS.md#f043), [F045](RAW_FACTS.md#f045), [F258](RAW_FACTS.md#f258).

Unchanged person/helmet boxes, classes and scores can assign one helmet to A correctly or B incorrectly: detector AP is identical while person violations invert. A helmet contained inside two overlapping person boxes can have intersection/helmet-area1 and IoU.02 for both: overlap alone cannot resolve ownership. **[MATH]** [F044](RAW_FACTS.md#f044).

Use GT rows known violation / known nonviolation / unknown; prediction columns violation / nonviolation / abstain / missed person. Full-chain violation recall=`correctly matched known violating people/all known violating GT people`; missed people and abstention on known violations remain misses. Precision over adjudicable assertions must show unknown/out-of-review assertions separately. Count unmatched/duplicate violating-person predictions as false only within sufficiently exhaustive ROI. Example: 100 known people, 20 violating; detect50 including5 violating, classify all50 correctly→matched-person accuracy100%, localization coverage50%, full-chain violation recall5/20=25%. **[PROPOSED/MATH]** [F046](RAW_FACTS.md#f046), [F047](RAW_FACTS.md#f047).

GT-person, GT-PPE and GT-owner substitutions, one at a time with the remainder fixed, can localize conditional sensitivity. Their gains are neither deployable improvements nor guaranteed upper bounds. Preserve negative/unexpected results and check downstream event regressions. **[PROPOSED/INFER]** [F200](RAW_FACTS.md#f200); [E003](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#e003), [Q017](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q017).

## Dangerous zones: image-anchor conformance is not physical intrusion

Choose the truth first: bottom-center pixel in polygon, person-image intersection, foot contact on calibrated floor, or named body intrusion into a3D volume. One foot inside versus midpoint inside can disagree. An exact implementation of a pixel rule supports only that rule unless physical correspondence is independently established. **[INFER/PROPOSED]** [F049](RAW_FACTS.md#f049), [F056](RAW_FACTS.md#f056).

OpenCV4.12.0 `pointPolygonTest` returns +1 inside, −1 outside, 0 on edge/vertex; `r>0` excludes boundary and `r>=0` includes it. Shapely2.1.2 point `contains` excludes boundary, `covers` includes it. Pixel distance is not meters. Empty/invalid/missing contour is configuration error/unknown, not verified outside. These were source/analytical checks; OpenCV/Shapely were not run. **[DOC/CODE/MATH]** [F050](RAW_FACTS.md#f050)–[F053](RAW_FACTS.md#f053), [F058](RAW_FACTS.md#f058).

A homography maps a plane. With camera height4m and point x=3m, z=2m, projecting its ray onto floor gives x=6m: accurate pixels/calibration cannot fix the wrong plane. Conversely, a true floor point and exactly projected polygon can support correct membership without a top-down image. Evaluate code conformance, calibration on withheld control points near working boundaries, GT-anchor membership and full predicted-person alert chain separately. Save coordinate transforms/crop/padding, zone/camera versions and validity; zoom/motion can invalidate the zone. Hidden feet remain unknown GT, and hysteresis/dwell must be checked for short-entry misses and delay. **[MATH/PROPOSED]** [F054](RAW_FACTS.md#f054)–[F060](RAW_FACTS.md#f060).

## Supplemental timely-event matching: lex-offline-v1 is a tiny exact oracle

Use this **proposed** endpoint only when GT identity, causal evidence, elapsed-clock mapping and required follow-up are known. It is separate from COCO, ActivityNet, sed_eval and future official scoring. Per GT store compatible camera/epoch/person-or-explicit-scene/type/zone-version keys, observable onset `s`, known active support `[s,e)`, first sufficient causal evidence `h`; per actual visible delivery store immutable ID, genuine consumed-evidence anchor `a` and delivery `d`. Physical onset, observable onset, evidence sufficiency, confirmation and delivery are distinct. Unit: common elapsed-clock seconds, never unrelated native PTS minus receiver monotonic time. **[PROPOSED]** [F258](RAW_FACTS.md#f258), [F259](RAW_FACTS.md#f259).

An eligible GT–alert edge requires compatible keys and independent person correspondence, `h<=a<=d`, anchor in that episode's known active support, `0<=d−s<=D`, and known active delivery `d<e`. Deadline equality passes; end equality fails. Owner chooses D and active-at-delivery utility; no numeric D is established. After-end reporting is a separate endpoint. Retrospective backdating cannot legitimize evidence-free early notification. Known short episodes remain eligible even if they end before D. **[PROPOSED]** [F259](RAW_FACTS.md#f259), [Q001](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q001).

For partial injective matching M minimize lexicographically `(-|M|,sum(cost),sorted_stable_ID_pairs)`. Cost=`d−s` for events,`1−IoU` for person boxes. Invalid edges are absent; unmatched vertices are explicit. Exact `Fraction`/ticks avoid arbitrary epsilon/big-M. Reference sorts immutable IDs, recursively skips/pairs GT with unused alerts and memoizes `(GT_index,used_alert_bitmask)`; it is exponential in alert count and suitable only for tiny correctness fixtures. A proposed scalable replacement must preserve maximum cardinality, then minimum cost at exactly that cardinality, then canonical tied pair list; merely calling Hungarian or sorting solver input does not prove equivalence. **[PROPOSED]** [F005](RAW_FACTS.md#f005), [F259](RAW_FACTS.md#f259); [Q016](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q016).

Actually executed: 13 named graphs and input permutations; all512 labeled3×3 graphs compared for cardinality with SciPy1.17.1 and all three priorities with an independent exhaustive partial-injection oracle;8 interval-edge cases, 4 censoring ledgers, wrong-person state and shared-onset examples. These finite checks do not validate imports, real GT, clock conversion, full external evaluators or scale. **[LOCAL]** [F260](RAW_FACTS.md#f260), [F261](RAW_FACTS.md#f261).

Sum-delay optimization does not minimize p95 or maximum. Onsets0, 2 and deliveries3, 4 can produce delays[3, 2] or[4, 1], both sum5 but different maxima. For fixed chosen subsets, sum(d−s)=sum(d)−sum(s) is permutation-invariant; canonical IDs can decide which delay distribution is reported. Offline reassignment scores frozen deliveries using later context; it neither changes real delivery time nor implements an online notification policy. **[MATH/INFER]** [F260](RAW_FACTS.md#f260), [F261](RAW_FACTS.md#f261).

Only for the **same known complete closed universe**: `TP=M`, `FN=N_G−M`, `FP=N_A−M`, `P=M/N_A`, `R=M/N_G`, `F1=2M/(N_A+N_G)`. Both empty→P/R/F1 NA; GT only→R0, P NA, F1=0; alerts only→P0, R NA, F1=0; latency has M samples, M0→NA. Compatible micro strata sum counts before division; undefined macro strata require an explicit policy. **[PROPOSED]** [F259](RAW_FACTS.md#f259).

Keep unmatched reasons/edge rejection flags: excess/duplicate, true-but-late, after-end, wrong person/type/zone, no GT, unresolved, out-of-scope. Reasons can overlap; predeclare precedence for disjoint totals. A semantically true late alert can create strict FP+FN while remaining true about the past. All visible records remain workload; strict unmatched does not mean semantically false. Report matched-delay quantiles+n jointly with misses, late outcomes, unknowns and coverage to expose survivor bias. **[PROPOSED]** [F072](RAW_FACTS.md#f072), [F172](RAW_FACTS.md#f172), [F259](RAW_FACTS.md#f259).

## Uncertain onset and censored follow-up: no midpoint TP or success-only denominator

Onset support `[l,u]` is a set of possible truths, not a uniform distribution or confidence interval. Raw delay=`[d−u,d−l]`; keep annotation, identity, clock-mapping and sampling uncertainty separate. For fixed causal/key facts, independent onset/end bounds `s∈[l,u]`,`e∈[v,w]`, s<e: a definite edge requires `u<=a`,`d−l<=D`,`d<v` and known active/identity truth in every allowed world; possible onset range is `L=max(l,d−D)` to `U=min(u,a,d)` with L<=U and some allowed e>d (simple closed bounds require d<w). Eligible delay range=`[d−U,d−L]`. Shared constraints can invalidate the independent-bound test; do not clamp a negative raw delay or choose a midpoint and call it observed. **[PROPOSED/DERIVATION]** [F262](RAW_FACTS.md#f262).

For fixed known G/A and coherent truth worlds Ω, definite-edge and possible-edge-union maximum cardinalities bound every world's count externally. A definite edge need not be selected; definite-only pair delays are not the chosen scorer's delay bounds. Possible-union maximum need not be attainable. Example: `s1=θ,s2=θ+1,θ∈[0,10]`, person-specific deliveries1 and11, D1. First pair needsθ∈[0, 1], secondθ∈[9, 10]; each is possible, union matches2, jointly feasible maximum1. Unknown roster can also change denominators. No general feasible-world onset/identity/roster/clock solver was created. **[PROPOSED/LOCAL]** [F006](RAW_FACTS.md#f006), [F262](RAW_FACTS.md#f262), [F263](RAW_FACTS.md#f263); [K005](CRITIC.md#k005) remains partially closed, [Q014](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q014) open.

A known episode s0, e2 and complete logs through2 is a complete short opportunity even when illustrative D5; no delivery is a miss, delivery at2 fails active-at-delivery. Unknown eventual end after6 does not censor a deadline5 endpoint observed completely through5. An event already active at clip start is pre-existing/left-censored, not incident onset0; notification from first sufficient evidence h can be a separate startup endpoint. **[PROPOSED]** [F264](RAW_FACTS.md#f264), [F265](RAW_FACTS.md#f265).

For s28, recording cutoffF30, D5, one delivery29 and one not-yet-delivered event: complete-follow-up primary has0 eligible events and recall NA, not100%. The incomplete stratum has2 events and1 observed success. A separately frozen observed-by-F endpoint includes both and gives1/2; full-D outcome remains unresolved. Remove entire incomplete eligibility strata and every potential-matching component touching unknown identity/roster/eligibility/follow-up, including successful pairs and alerts, to prevent competition changing the remainder. Disclose excluded counts/exposure; this narrow complete-component endpoint is not unbiased whole-footage recall. Keep semantic false/workload ledgers independently. Known GT plus complete delivery logs during processing outage can establish operational FN; missing recording/GT/logs is unresolved. **[PROPOSED/LOCAL]** [F264](RAW_FACTS.md#f264), [F265](RAW_FACTS.md#f265).
## False delivered records per camera-hour: review sufficiency depends on the estimand

## Remote latency, clocks and replay: name start and end events

Measure completed work and distinguish queue delay from computation. `submit_return−submit` may be enqueue overhead; `output_ready−submit` is completed call inside those boundaries; `output_ready−scheduled` includes dispatch lateness/backlog; `decision_ready−received` is receiver pipeline excluding upstream capture buffering. Event delivery delay needs independent GT onset and actual destination boundary. No single FPS substitutes for them. **[PROPOSED/INFER]** [F146](RAW_FACTS.md#f146), [F155](RAW_FACTS.md#f155), [F156](RAW_FACTS.md#f156).

Use documented same-system monotonic/performance counters for elapsed time; nanosecond units do not establish nanosecond accuracy. Never subtract clocks with unrelated monotonic origins. RTP has rate/offset; RTCP NTP mapping does not itself guarantee true capture UTC. Without camera timestamp semantics and verified mapping/error, physical frame age is unknown even if receiver residence is measurable. A recently received frame may have spent10s upstream;20ms inference does not erase that age. **[DOC/INFER]** [F153](RAW_FACTS.md#f153)–[F157](RAW_FACTS.md#f157).

Unpaced file replay measures corpus processing speed with separate source duration/wall duration. Paced replay uses actual host due schedule: constant speed s, `due(p)=m0+(p−p0)/s`; `delivery−due` is replay lag, not live physical age. Save piecewise speed/pause schedule. Executed arithmetic: start1000, speed1 until PTS50 then2; PTS80 due1065, delivery1068→lag3; using stale speed1 mapping gives false−12. Source exposure unchanged, wall workload changes. **[PROPOSED/LOCAL]** [F157](RAW_FACTS.md#f157), [F233](RAW_FACTS.md#f233).

For every timestamp retain ID, unit, clock domain, event definition and provenance: sourcePTS/capture, scheduled, received, decoded, submit, submit_return, completion_observed, output_ready, decision_ready, durable delivery and instrumented presentation. ## Load, failures, freshness and coverage: do not let lost work improve the score

Create an expected-work manifest independent of successful outputs. Keep source frames, scheduled logical analysis opportunities, request attempts, batches, logical results and visible alerts as separate units. Retries add attempts, not unique opportunities; each batched input retains its own original scheduled release and batch-fill waiting. NominalFPS×duration need not equal frames actually emitted by a live camera. Unknown emission denominator must be called scheduled/requested camera-hours, not supplied source-hours. Track not_received, decode_failed, planned sampling, overload drop and unresolved separately. **[PROPOSED]** [F169](RAW_FACTS.md#f169), [F229](RAW_FACTS.md#f229).

At cutoff each attempt has one status: valid response, explicit error, timeout/cancel or unresolved; late response is a linked later observation. Logical opportunities separately record timely valid, late valid, failed without valid, dropped, unresolved. Valid empty detections are results; no response is not. Publish offered/admitted/unique frames/s, batches/s, attempt counts and completion fractions with denominators. **[PROPOSED]** [F169](RAW_FACTS.md#f169), [F230](RAW_FACTS.md#f230).

p50/p95/p99 among successful calls need n, workload, boundaries and adjacent error/timeout/pending rates plus timely-valid/all-selected logical opportunities. Slow requests timing out can improve success-only p95 while service worsens. Do not invent zero/infinite measured latency for failures; time-to-timeout is another endpoint. Do not average per-camera p99 or sum component p99 into end-to-end p99. Proposed harness nearest-rank percentile=`x[ceil(p*n)]` (1-based sorted samples, no interpolation); pin convention, small-n tails may be maxima. **[PROPOSED/INFER]** [F156](RAW_FACTS.md#f156), [F172](RAW_FACTS.md#f172).

Preserve source-time masks R=sufficient independent review for the named metric, O=observability, A=completed analysis under a declared temporal-support rule, F=fresh result under a chosen criterion. They intersect, not sum. One processed frame does not cover the interval until the next by default. Without justified sample→time support, publish counts and gap distributions instead of invented analyzed hours. Report per-camera requested/scheduled, supplied, analyzed, fresh exposure; `H_A/H_requested`, `H_F/H_requested`, longest and inter-result gaps. Endpoint uptime, analysis availability and fresh useful availability differ. Conditional quality on completed support is diagnostic; full-requested-footage timely recall requires independent GT/logs throughout requested footage. **[PROPOSED]** [F170](RAW_FACTS.md#f170), [F171](RAW_FACTS.md#f171).

False/requested-hour improves1→.1 while recall.8→.1 and coverage100%→10%; analyzed-time false rate stays1/hour. If the other9 hours were never recorded/truth-known, report evidence/coverage loss rather than inventing9FN. FIFO arrival30frames/s, service20frames/s also produces≈10frames/s backlog despite stable50ms computation. Latest-frame drops may restore freshness while losing short events; show both. **[MATH/INFER]** [F158](RAW_FACTS.md#f158), [F171](RAW_FACTS.md#f171), [F234](RAW_FACTS.md#f234).

Run a bounded predeclared ladder: baseline→increased offered load→overload→fault→recovery→drain/close. Vary streams, cadence/resolution, batch/in-flight or bursts one at a time before needed interactions. Separate initialization/load/first result, cold start and actual warmup/steady state. Wait-for-completion loops hide offered-arrival queues; use paced arrivals when testing them. Fix model, GT and temporal policy across cells. Repeating one video isolates scheduling changes, not new independent camera samples. Recovery must name last fresh observation, fault boundary, retries/reset/stale replies, first valid new-epoch frame, first fresh useful result and restored cadence. Catching up backlog can restore completeness before freshness; discarding it does the reverse. **[PROPOSED]** [F158](RAW_FACTS.md#f158), [F168](RAW_FACTS.md#f168), [F172](RAW_FACTS.md#f172); [E007](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#e007), [Q008](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q008), [Q013](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q013).
