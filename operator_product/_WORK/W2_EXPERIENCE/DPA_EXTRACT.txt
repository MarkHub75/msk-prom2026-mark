EXHIBIT A | Protex AI Data Processing Addendum  
This Data Processing Addendum (“DPA”) is supplementary to and shall be construed in accordance with the Agreement.  
1.​
Definitions 
1.1.​
Capitalized terms not otherwise defined in the Agreement shall have the following meanings: 
“Authority” means for Personal Data originating in: (a) the EEA, the European Commission; (b) the UK, the Information 
Commissioner’s Office (or its successor); and (c) anywhere else, the appropriate data protection authority of competent 
jurisdiction pursuant to the Data Protection Laws.  
“Client Personal Data” means the Personal Data that is shared by the Client with Protex AI in connection with the Offering.  
“Controller” has the meaning given to it in the Data Protection Laws. 
“Data Protection Impact Assessment” has the meaning given to it in the Data Protection Laws. 
“Data Protection Laws” means all data protection and privacy laws, including guidance issued by any applicable data 
protection authority, applicable to any Personal Data, as may be amended or replaced from time to time, including without 
limitation: (a) in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 (the "EU GDPR") and the Privacy and 
Electronic Communications Directive 2002/58/EC (as the same may be superseded by the Regulation on Privacy and Electronic 
Communications); (b) in the UK, the Data Protection Act 2018 as amended and the "UK GDPR" as that term is defined in the 
Act; and (c) in the United States of America, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights 
Act 2020. 
“Data Subject” has the meaning given to it in the Data Protection Laws. 
“EEA” means the European Economic Area.  
“EU Controller-to-Processor Clauses” means the standard contractual clauses between controllers and processors for data 
transfers to Third Countries, as approved by the European Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021, 
available at www.protex.ai/legal (Standard Contractual Clauses). 
“EU Processor-to-Processor Clauses” means the standard contractual clauses between processors for data transfers to Third 
Countries, as approved by the European Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021, available at 
www.protex.ai/legal (Standard Contractual Clauses). 
“GDPR Laws” means the EU GDPR and UK GDPR, collectively. 
“International Data Transfer Addendum” or “IDTA” means the International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU 
Processor-to-Processor Clauses and the EU Controller-to-Processor Clauses as approved by the Information Commissioner’s 
Office of the United Kingdom under section 119A(1) of the Data Protection Act 2018, available at www.protex.ai/legal (Standard 
Contractual Clauses). 
“Personal Data” has the meaning given to it in the Data Protection Laws.  
“Personal Data Breach” means any breach of security or other action or inaction leading to the accidental or unlawful 
destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Personal Data by Protex AI, its affiliates, sub-processors, 
or any other identified or unidentified third party. Any other identified or unidentified third party does not include Client Affiliates or 
Outsource Providers.  
“Processor” has the meaning given to it in the Data Protection Laws.  
“Standard Contractual Clauses” means the EU Processor-to-Processor Clauses, EU Controller-to-Processor Clauses and the 
International Data Transfer Addendum, as applicable, all available at www.protex.ai/legal (Standard Contractual Clauses).

“Third Country” means in respect of Personal Data originating in: (a) the EEA, a country outside of the EEA not recognized by 
the European Commission as providing an adequate level of protection for Personal Data (as described in the EU GDPR); and 
(b) the UK, a country outside the UK not recognized by the Information Commissioner's Office as providing an adequate level of 
protection for Personal Data (as described in the UK GDPR). 
“TOMs” means the technical and organizational measures contained in Schedule 1.  
2.​
Data Processing 
2.1.​
Scope and Roles. This DPA applies when Protex AI processes Client Personal Data under the Agreement. In this context, Client 
is the Controller and Protex AI is the Processor. Each party agrees that it will comply with all Data Protection Laws in exercising 
its rights and performing its obligations under this Agreement, as such laws apply to a Controller and Processor respectively. 
2.2.​
Details of the Processing. 
(a)​
Subject matter: The subject matter for the processing under this DPA is Client Personal Data. 
(b)​
Duration: Client Personal Data will be processed under this DPA for the Term and in accordance with the Agreement. 
(c)​
Nature and purpose: Protex AI will process Client Personal Data to provide, operate and maintain the Offering. 
(d)​
Categories of Data Subject: The categories of Data Subject, whose Personal Data may be processed by Protex AI as 
Client Personal Data include: (i) individuals at or who visit a Client Site covered by Client-side cameras connected to the 
Protex AI Offering, which may include Client's employees, temporary workers, agents and volunteers and independent 
contractors engaged by the Client (“Client Site Visitors”); and (ii) users of the Protex AI Offering, which may include 
Client’s employees and those of its Affiliates. 
(e)​
Types of Personal Data: The types of Personal Data that Protex AI may process include: (i) user account information, 
such as user full names, job title, phone numbers, business email addresses, account information, authentication 
information, usage data (e.g., features used and actions taken), information collected via support and Protex Intelligence 
functions (including support emails, user chat, responses and feedback); (ii) user device information, such as device 
used, browser and browser version, operating system and version, IP address, and geo-location data; (iii) video / image 
data of Client’s Site Visitors; and (iv) any other Personal Data contained in Client Personal Data submitted through the 
Offering by Client.  
3.​
Instructions 
3.1.​
Documented Instructions. The parties agree that this DPA and the Agreement (including any instructions provided by Client to 
Protex AI required for or related to the Offering) constitute Client's documented instructions regarding Protex AI’s processing of 
Client Personal Data ("Documented Instructions"). 
3.2.​
Compliance with Documented Instructions. Protex AI will only process Client Personal Data in accordance with Documented 
Instructions unless required to do so by applicable law, in which case Protex AI will, to the extent legally permissible, inform 
Client of that legal requirement before processing. Protex AI shall promptly inform Client if, in Protex AI’s opinion, an instruction 
from Client infringes the Data Protection Laws. Client acknowledges that any such opinion is not legal advice and Client shall not 
rely on it.  
3.3.​
Change of Scope. If the Documented Instructions require Protex AI to perform actions that go beyond its obligations under this 
DPA or the scope of work for the Offering set out in the Agreement, Protex AI shall inform the Client and require the Client to 
provide different Documented Instructions, the carrying out of which will fall within the scope of the Offering or within the scope 
of Protex AI’s obligations under this DPA. 
4.​
Confidentiality 
4.1.​
Personnel. Protex AI will take reasonable steps to ensure the reliability of any persons authorized to process any Client Personal 
Data and shall ensure that all such persons have committed themselves to written obligations of confidentiality.

5.​
Security 
5.1.​
Technical and Organizational Measures. Considering the nature, scope, context and purposes of processing, Protex AI has 
implemented and will maintain for the Term, the administrative, physical, technical and organizational measures as set out in the 
TOMs (as attached at Schedule 1) to protect any Client Personal Data accessed or processed by it against unauthorized or 
unlawful processing or accidental loss, destruction, damage or disclosure. 
5.2.​
Client Approval of TOMs. The parties agree that for the purposes of processing Client Personal Data under this DPA and the 
Agreement, the measures contained within the TOMs are appropriate, given the nature of the data to be processed and the 
harm that might result from such unauthorized or unlawful processing or accidental loss, destruction, disclosure, access or 
damage. 
5.3.​
Information Security Management System. Protex AI has been certified as operating an Information Security Management 
System which complies with the requirements of ISO 27001 (ISO/IEC 27001:2022), and Protex AI will continue to maintain such 
certification (or equivalent) for the duration of the Term. 
6.​
Sub-Processing 
6.1.​
Consent Needed. Subject to Section 6.2 (Authorization of Sub-Processors), Protex AI shall not without the prior written consent 
of Client, engage any sub-processors for the processing of Client Personal Data under this Agreement. 
6.2.​
Authorization of Sub-Processors. Client consents to and authorizes: (a) Protex AI’s Affiliates, and (b) the parties listed in 
Schedule 2 (Approved Sub-Processors) to act as sub-processors for Protex AI in the provision of the Offering and on terms 
materially equivalent to those contained in this DPA (“Approved Sub-Processors”). Protex AI shall be fully liable for any breach 
by the sub-processors of any of the obligations contained in this DPA. 
7.​
Cross-Border Transfers 
7.1.​
Consent to Transfer Required. Subject to Section 7.2 (Cloud Provider), if Client Personal Data originates in: (a) the EEA, or (b) 
the UK, Protex AI will not transfer such Client Personal Data to a Third Country, without the prior written consent of Client and 
not without procuring provision of adequate safeguards (as defined by relevant Authority from time to time) in accordance with 
applicable Data Protection Laws. 
7.2.​
Cloud Provider; Sub-Processors. Client Personal Data may be hosted by the Cloud Provider in the Hosted Location agreed by 
the parties and may be transferred to the Approved Sub-Processors located in a Third Country. Client acknowledges and 
consents to such processing of Client Personal Data outside of: (a) the EEA, and (b) the UK, solely and to the extent necessary 
for Protex AI to provide the Offering and for which purposes the relevant Standard Contractual Clauses shall apply. 
7.3.​
Standard Contractual Clauses - Client is Data Exporter. When Client is acting as a Controller and transfers Client Personal Data 
originating in the:  
(a)​
EEA, to a Processor located in a Third Country, the EU Controller-to-Processor Clauses will apply; and 
(b)​
UK, to a Processor located in a Third Country, the Information Commissioner’s Office of the United Kingdom’s 
International Data Transfer Addendum will apply. 
7.4.​
Standard Contractual Clauses - Protex AI is Data Exporter. When Protex AI is acting as a Processor and transfers Client 
Personal Data originating in the: 
(a)​
EEA, to a Processor located in a Third Country, the EU Processor-to-Processor Clauses will apply; and 
(b)​
UK, to a Processor located in a Third Country, the Information Commissioner’s Office of the United Kingdom’s 
International Data Transfer Addendum will apply. 
7.5.​
Change in Law. The parties agree that Protex AI, may at its sole discretion, update and or entirely replace paragraphs 7.3(b) 
and 7.4(b) of this DPA, which concern transfers of Client Personal Data originating from the UK to a Third Country, if the 
Information Commissioner's Office (or its successor) provides an alternative or replacement recognized compliance standard for 
such transfers in accordance with the UK GDPR, to that already stated in this DPA. Protex AI will provide notice of such change 
in writing to the Client.

8.1.​
Notice of Requests. Protex AI will use reasonable efforts to promptly notify Client if it receives: (a) a request from a Data Subject 
to have access to that person’s Personal Data; (b) a complaint or request relating to Client’s obligations under the Data 
Protection Laws; or (c) any other communication relating directly or indirectly to the processing of any Personal Data in

8.2.​
Protex AI Assistance. Considering the nature of processing and the information available to Protex AI, Protex AI will provide 
reasonable support to the Client in:  
(a)​
complying with any legally mandated request for access to or correction of any Personal Data by a Data Subject under 
Chapter III of each of the GDPR Laws (and where such request is submitted to Protex AI, Protex AI will promptly notify 
Client of it); 
(b)​
responding to requests or demands made to Client by any court or governmental authority responsible for enforcing the 
Data Protection Laws; and 
(c)​
its preparation of a Data Protection Impact Assessment. 
9.​
Personal Data Breach 
9.1.​
Notice of Personal Data Breach. In the event that Protex AI suffers or becomes aware of a Personal Data Breach it will inform 
Client without undue delay, on becoming aware of the same, and will take reasonable steps to mitigate the effects and to 
minimize any damages resulting from such breach. 
9.2.​
Information. In the event of a Personal Data Breach, Protex AI (to the extent reasonably possible), will provide the following 
information to Client: 
(a)​
a description of the nature of the incident, including where possible the categories and appropriate number of Data 
Subjects concerned and the categories and approximate number of Personal Data records concerned; 
(b)​
the name and contact details of the relevant Processor’s data protection officer or another contact point where more 
information can be obtained; 
(c)​
a description of the likely consequences of the incident; and 
(d)​
a description of the measures taken and / or proposed to be taken by the relevant Processor to address the incident 
including, where appropriate, measures to mitigate possible adverse effects. 
Protex AI may provide such information in stages, or further update it, as investigations progress.  
10.​
Audit 
10.1.​ Documentation. Protex AI agrees to maintain its ISO 27001 certification for the duration of the Term. Protex AI will use an 
external auditor to verify that its security measures meet ISO 27001 standards in accordance with the ISO certification process. 
On Client's written request, and subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations, Protex AI will provide to Client: (a) a copy of its 
then-current certificate in relation to its ISO 27001 certification; and (b) any information reasonably requested by Client 
concerning Protex AI’s processing of Client Personal Data under the Agreement and this DPA.  
10.2.​ Audit Scope. Other than in the context of investigating a Personal Data Breach involving Client Personal Data, Client agrees to 
exercise any right it may have to conduct an audit or inspection under Data Protection Laws (or the Standard Contractual 
Clauses, if applicable) by requesting the information outlined in Section 10.1 (Documentation). Client acknowledges that the 
Cloud Provider does not  permit, and certain other of Protex AI’s subprocessors may not permit, audits, in which case Client may 
refer to the information published by Cloud Provider or the relevant subprocessor concerning those entities’ processing 
activities.

Schedule 1 | TOMs 
1.​
Technical Measures 
1.1.​
Access Controls.  
(a)​
Access to systems processing Client Data is restricted using role-based access control (RBAC). 
(b)​
Administrative access is restricted to authorized personnel and protected using multi-factor authentication (MFA). 
(c)​
Access permissions are reviewed regularly and revoked promptly upon role changes or termination. 
1.2.​
Device-Level Processing and Protection.  
(a)​
Client has the option to configure Protex AI Devices to apply irreversible anonymisation (e.g., facial blurring or ghosting) 
to locally processed video feeds prior to data transmission to the Protex AI backend cloud infrastructure. 
(b)​
Protex AI may temporarily process short video segments containing unblurred footage in the cloud for the sole purposes 
of: (i) quality assurance of event detection; and (ii) training machine learning models to detect general environmental 
features (e.g., persons, objects, equipment) and fine tuning of such models. Such footage is not used to identify 
individuals. It is securely stored, access-controlled, and privacy features are applied (e.g., facial blurring) as early as 
practicable during processing.  
(c)​
Only short, event-based video clips and image frames are retained, encrypted using AES-256, and uploaded to the 
secure backend. 
(d)​
Devices are deployed with outbound-only network configurations and do not accept inbound connections. 
1.3.​
Encryption in Transit and at Rest.  
(a)​
All Client Data in transit is secured using SSL/TLS encryption protocols. 
(b)​
Client Data at rest, including video, metadata, and database records, is encrypted using AES-256 or equivalent. 
(c)​
Encryption keys are managed via the Cloud Provider’s key management service. 
1.4.​
Network Security.  
(a)​
AWS Web Application Firewall (WAF) and AWS Shield are deployed to protect against web threats. 
(b)​
Security groups enforce a deny-by-default policy and restrict traffic to specific ports and IPs. 
(c)​
Network architecture is segmented into public, private, and data subnets with no direct public access to sensitive 
components. 
1.5.​
Identity and Session Management. 
(a)​
Authentication supports both SAML and OAuth 2.0-compliant Identity Providers. 
(b)​
Authentication tokens are short-lived and securely stored; session management is handled via AWS Cognito and Amplify. 
(c)​
MFA is available through both Client IdPs and Cognito. 
1.6.​
Authorization and Data Access Control.  
(a)​
Permissions are enforced throughout the application using role-based access control.​
 
(b)​
Backend and data layers restrict access down to row and column levels where applicable.

1.7.​
Monitoring, Logging and Retention.  
(a)​
System access and activity are logged and monitored using AWS-native tooling. 
(b)​
Default retention for Client Data is 30 days post-termination. Clients may download their Client Data manually or via the 
API at any time, and prior to closing their accounts. 
(c)​
Secure deletion and erasure mechanisms are in place for expired or terminated data. 
1.8.​
Backup & Disaster Recovery.  
(a)​
Automated, encrypted backups are performed regularly to a separate region within the same country. 
(b)​
Backup integrity is monitored; failures trigger alerts and incident responses. 
1.9.​
Privacy By Design.  
(a)​
Facial recognition is not used and footage is not used to identify individuals; unblurred footage is irreversibly anonymised 
as early as possible during processing.  
(b)​
Metadata, rather than identifiable imagery, is leveraged for analytics. 
(c)​
System design prioritises facility-level insights over individual identification. 
2.​
Organizational Measures 
2.1.​
Security Governance.  
(a)​
Protex AI maintains an ISO/IEC 27001-certified Information Security Management System (ISMS). 
(b)​
External audits are conducted to ensure ongoing compliance and effectiveness. 
2.2.​
Confidentiality.  
(a)​
Staff accessing Personal Data are subject to written obligations of confidentiality and background checks. 
(b)​
Security training is mandatory during onboarding and conducted regularly thereafter. 
2.3.​
Subprocessor Oversight.  
(a)​
Subprocessors are reviewed for security and privacy compliance. 
(b)​
Data Processing Agreements are in place to ensure adherence to relevant legal and contractual standards. 
2.4.​
Incident Response.  
(a)​
A structured incident response process is maintained for identifying, managing, and reporting Personal Data Breaches. 
2.5.​
Data Subject Rights. 
(a)​
Protex AI supports Clients in responding to data subject rights requests including access, correction, and deletion.​
